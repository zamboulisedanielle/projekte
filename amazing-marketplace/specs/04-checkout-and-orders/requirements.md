# Feature 04 – Checkout, Invoice, Order History

## Goal
Customers order the cart to a delivery address, receive an invoice and see all their orders.

## Requirements
- Checkout form: name, street, ZIP (4–5 digits), city, country. No payment step.
- The cart is split into one order per shop; stock is reduced; the cart is emptied.
- Each order gets a unique order number.
- Invoice PDF with order number, date, customer, delivery address, items, net amount, 7 % VAT, shipping and total.
- Order history lists all orders with status and an invoice button.
