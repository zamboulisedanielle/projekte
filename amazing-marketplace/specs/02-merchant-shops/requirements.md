# Feature 02 – Merchant: Shops and Offers

## Goal
Merchants manage their own shops and decide which books they sell, at what price and with how much stock.

## Requirements
- A merchant can create, edit and delete own shops (name, city, description).
- A shop with orders cannot be deleted (order history must stay intact).
- A merchant offers a book either from the existing catalogue or by creating a new book (title, author, ISBN, year, description).
- The same book can only be offered once per shop.
- Price and stock can be changed directly in the offer table; offers can be removed.
- A merchant can never see or change another merchant's shops or offers.
