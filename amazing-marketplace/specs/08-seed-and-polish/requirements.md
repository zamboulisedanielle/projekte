# Feature 08 – Seed Data, Polish, Screenshots

## Goal
Every feature can be demonstrated right after setup, and the UI looks professional.

## Requirements
- Seed: 24 books, 4 shops, 2 merchants, 3 customers, orders over the last 30 days, items in a cart.
- Consistent design: bookbinding palette (navy cloth, gilt accent), Literata for headings, generated typographic book covers.
- Responsive down to mobile; visible keyboard focus.
- Screenshots of all main screens in `Screenshots/`.
