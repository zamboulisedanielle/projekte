# Feature 03 – Customer: Search and Cart

## Goal
Customers find books quickly and collect them in a cart.

## Requirements
- Search by title, author or ISBN; results update while typing.
- Each result shows all shops offering the book, with price and stock.
- Customers add an offer to the cart; quantity cannot exceed stock.
- The cart groups items by shop, allows changing quantities and removing items, and shows the total.
- A badge in the toolbar shows the number of items.
- Guests see a hint to log in; merchants and admins cannot use the cart.
